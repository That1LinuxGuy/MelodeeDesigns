We are an Alabama local costume rental shop. Melodee Allen has over 20 years of experience in costume design and continues to learn and perfect her craft every day. 

Melodee Allen has costumed award winning shows in Fort Worth Texas, supporting the prestigious [Betty Buckley](https://casamanana.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Buckley-Winners-Archive-Updated-2026.pdf) awards for both The Sound of Music and Fiddler on the Roof at Covenant Christian Academy in 2015 and 2017 respectively.
