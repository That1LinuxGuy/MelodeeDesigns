Q. Is Melodee Designs able to costume a complete theater production?
{{< /lead >}}

A. *Yes! We have outfitted entire casts for High School, College and Community Theater Productions. We have thousands of pieces in stock and our inventory is constantly expanding. With our **sustainable** fashion approach, we source heavily from post-consumer pieces that we modify to create pieces that will fit your show. Plus we have the skill and resources to craft pieces from scratch when needed.*

{{< lead >}}
Q. I only need a few pieces. Will Melodee Designs rent individual characters or just the pieces I need?
{{< /lead >}}

A. *Yes! We want to help you complete the look of your show even if that means we only do a character or two or help out with a chorus number. We love to work with a director to craft a one-of-a-kind piece for the Lead’s “big number.” But on the other hand we have loads of basics that can help you complete a scene. Need dozens of Newsboy caps? We have them! Need a white shirt and black bow tie for every guy in your cast? We can help with that! Contact us and let us know what you need.*

{{< lead >}}
Q. I’m not in theater; I just want a costume for an event. Does Melodee Designs rent to individuals?
{{< /lead >}}

A. *Yes! We love helping people find the right look for a Renaissance Fair, holiday party or other event. Give us a call so we can hear your ideas and find just the right look for you!*

{{< lead >}}
Q. What areas do you serve?
{{< /lead >}}

A. *A. We are centrally located in downtown Decatur, Alabama just minutes from I-65, US Highways 31 and 72 and AL Highway 20. For theatrical productions we serve all of North Alabama and southern Central Tennessee. If you’re outside that area – or you’re not sure if you’re in or out – please contact us. We love costuming theater productions so don’t assume you’re too far away.*

{{< lead >}}
Q. What should we expect when we rent from Melodee Designs for our production?
{{< /lead >}}

A. *At Melodee Designs we want to be the resource that best fits your needs. That starts with the foundation of our service - the consultation. We will meet with you in person, by phone or video chat to discuss your project, what you need and how you see Melodee Designs involvement. Whether Melodee Designs measures the cast and creates the whole look of your show or a few stock pieces are delivered to you is up to you. By tech week you will have everything you need sorted, labeled and with a detailed packing list in hand and ready for dress rehearsals.*

{{< lead >}}
Q. What should we expect when we rent from Melodee Designs for our production?
{{< /lead >}}

A. *here are three basic options for transporting costumes. Pick up by the renter (you), delivery by Melodee Designs, or shipping by common carrier such as UPS, FEDX or USPS paid by the renter.*

*Since first fittings are an especially important part of costuming, we recommend letting Melodee Designs deliver your costumes. For an additional mileage fee, we will deliver the costumes to your facility at a time when your cast can try everything on. We always bring alternative pieces in case our first options don’t work out. Once that fitting is complete we will leave the pieces that work and return at a later time with anything that needs to be added to complete the show.*

{{< lead >}}
Q. Are we allowed to alter the garments?
{{< /lead >}}

A. *Yes. Simple alterations to fit an actor are allowed. We request that you use methods that are easily reversible so that upon return the garment can be reverted back to its original shape. Any damage done to garments in the course of alteration or any modifications that are not reversible may incur a damage fee.*
